Where SFT helped, and where it cost. Differences are called real only where they clear roughly two standard errors at the stated n; everything else is sampling noise even at full dataset. Gained: PT-Culture in chat mode (0.1884 → 0.2730, ~14 SE — the one large, unambiguous win, and it is the benchmark with real multi-turn chat structure); ChatRAG-Hi in raw mode (+0.021, ~4 SE); Cocoteros-es from 0.00 to 1.44 BLEU. Cost: OpenBookQA-es (−0.096, ~5 SE), CALAME-PT (−0.029, ~2.7 SE), and — most sharply — translation, where Phrases loses 2.8 and 4.0 BLEU in the two directions. Noise: Belebele, COPA, XStoryCloze, Portugal Basic QA (n=50), MMLU in all three languages, and every math benchmark.
Three cells that are not what they look like. (1) EsCoLA reads 0.3200 → 0.7094 and means nothing. Matthews correlation is 0.068 for SFT and −0.018 in chat mode, i.e. no correlation with the labels at all: both checkpoints emit a near-constant class and EsCoLA is ~2/3 one label, so the accuracy column is class imbalance. It is struck through above. (2) CALAME-PT and Phrases have no chat column — not because they were skipped, but because the format is invalid for them: CALAME-PT is last-word completion and Phrases is an ICL translation pattern, and asked in chat form the model returns an empty turn (99.9% of CALAME-PT generations were empty, scoring 0.0005). That is a measurement artifact, so it is not reported as a regression. (3) ALBA is still unscored (no ANTHROPIC_API_KEY): 800 generations were produced and saved with no judge_score. It is the one open-ended instruction-following benchmark here, which makes it the measurement most relevant to an SFT model, and it is the one still missing.
Which benchmarks get a chat column. The chat template is applied only where the prompt is already a question or an instruction and the target is a free response: ALBA, PT-Culture, ChatRAG-Hi, GSM8K-pt/hi, MATH-en, MGSM-es, MMLU-pt/es/hi, Portugal Basic QA, Belebele-es and EsCoLA. It is withheld from continuation-shaped tasks — CALAME-PT (bare fragment, predict the last word), COPA-es (premise + "porque"), XStoryCloze-es (story continuation), OpenBookQA-es (a bare question stem) and Phrases ("Oració en espanyol: X" → translation). The split was decided from each task's prompt template, and the evidence agrees: every benchmark that collapsed to zero under the chat template is on the withheld list. One unexplained case: Cocoteros-es is instruction-shaped ("Genera una frase corta con estas palabras…") yet scores 1.44 BLEU raw and 0.00 in chat. Its chat number is withheld pending an explanation — the lm-evaluation-harness adapter does not log per-example predictions, so --log-samples cannot show why.

What it looks like

Same prompt, same seed, same decoding — base checkpoint vs SFT checkpoint. The behavioural change is unambiguous; the factual quality is unchanged.

Behaviour: changed

the thing SFT was for

base · chat · “Olá! Como estás?” Não tenho nada a dizer quanto a mim / Mas o que quer que eu diga, é que já me falaram que não vou ir embora… (runs to the token limit, never stops)
SFT · chat · “Olá! Como estás?” Olá! Estou bem, obrigado por perguntar. E você, como está? 😊 (stops on <|eot_id|>)

Corroborating signal: the SFT eval runs took a third of the base model's wall clock, because the model stops instead of generating to max_gen_toks.

Knowledge: unchanged

and now delivered more confidently

SFT · greedy · “Qual é a capital de Portugal?” A capital de Portugal é Porto de Ave. — never Lisboa, across 5 seeds
SFT · greedy · “Qual é a capital de França?” A capital de França é Paris. — retrievable facts do surface, cleanly formatted
base · raw continuation · “Lisboa é a capital de…” …Portugal, e é uma das cidades mais importantes para a economia portuguesa.

The association exists, but only in that direction — neither checkpoint answers the question form. A reversal-curse failure inherited from pretraining, not caused by SFT. SFT did not lose the fact; it was never retrievable.

Corpus note. 154,049 of 316,124 rendered conversations (48.7%) were dropped for exceeding the 1,024-token context window — a hard ceiling from the base model's learned positional embeddings, not a tunable. The filter is not uniform: magpie_ultra, the multi-turn general-instruction source, lost 88% of its rows; smol_rewrite lost 2. Hindi is 35.3% of surviving tokens, but 94% of that is aya_hi's two templated tasks (headline→article, article→summary); indic_instruct_hi, the only general-purpose Hindi source, is 2.0%. ChatRAG-Hi is the one generative benchmark that gets worse after SFT, and Hindi chat replies are qualitatively incoherent where pt/es are clean — three independent measurements pointing at the same gap.
